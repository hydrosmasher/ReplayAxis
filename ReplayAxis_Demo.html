ReplayAxis Assistant

Type a command or press the microphone and say “ReplayAxis, pause”, “next frame”, or “ask what this case shows”.

Browser speech recognition may use an online service. Camera recognition runs in the browser after loading its model. Hold an open palm to play, a fist to pause, a thumbs-up to step forward, or a thumbs-down to step back. Release the pose before repeating.

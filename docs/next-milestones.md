Status snapshot · 19 November 2026

The active phase is research only. Each checkpoint has a concrete output and a decision.

| Checkpoint | Work | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Supervisor scope | Review the [thesis brief](thesis-feasibility-brief.md) and decide whether measurement validation or reproducibility is the primary contribution. Confirm the running population, event and use case. | One primary research question, one initial metric and a clear boundary for cricket and XR are recorded. |

| 4. Acceptance criteria | Use the [worksheet](threshold-worksheet-and-reference-checklist.md) to collect practical thresholds and qualify reference uncertainty. | Intended decision, error limits, coverage minima, sample/interval plan and reference uncertainty are frozen before held-out scoring. |
| 5. Prespecify evaluation | Finalize the [validation protocols](endpoint-validation-protocols.md) and [decision table](preregistration-decision-table.md), including timing sensitivity and independent reproduction of a selected metric. | Data split, processing settings, trial eligibility, denominator, uncertainty reporting and success rules are versioned. |
| 6. Prepare the pilot and funding case | Map remaining gaps to the smallest qualified pilot, using existing data where appropriate. Document requested resources, expected evidence and grounds to stop or narrow the work. | Supervisor can decide whether to proceed with a software-only evaluation, seek a scoped capture trial or defer the endpoint. |

Eligible PDFs are an ancillary documentation task. Keep citations and links available while verifying redistribution permissions and access. Hardware selection, new athlete recording and broader feature work belong to a later phase after the evidence review and relevant approvals.

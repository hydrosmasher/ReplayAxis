# Final project goals

Status snapshot · 19 November 2026

**North star.** An analyst can inspect a 3D sports replay, select a measurement and trace it back to the synchronized observations and processing decisions that produced it. When the evidence cannot support a position or an angle, the system says so.

| Horizon | Goal | Evidence of completion |
|---|---|---|
| Thesis | Validate sagittal knee flexion at force-defined running initial contact in a bounded capture setting. Demonstrate an independent reconstruction of a selected measurement from its evidence export. | Qualified synchronized reference, frozen error and coverage criteria, held-out evaluation and documented independent reproduction within the declared numerical tolerance. |
| Transfer | Test whether the same evidence workflow supports metric pre-bounce ball flight, with a separate cricket reference and scope. | Independent aligned 3D reference, error and coverage by flight phase, and traceability from displayed ball position to its source observations. |
| Spatial review | Deliver a usable desktop and optional spatial replay where synchronized camera evidence, uncertainty and rejection decisions are easy to inspect. | Representative reviewers correctly identify and reproduce a result and recognize unsupported estimates, compared with a baseline with equivalent information. |
| Voice and gesture | Support commands such as pointing to a joint, asking for a comparison and correcting which side, event or athlete was selected. | Measured recognition and correction rates, task completion time and evidence access in a controlled interaction comparison. |
| Forecasting | Evaluate motion beyond observation as a conditional estimate, including plausible alternatives when supported. | Results on unseen trajectories against simple baselines, calibrated uncertainty by horizon, and a clear visual distinction between reconstructed and predicted data. |
| Distribution | Package a reproducible system with documented formats, independent rebuild instructions, permissions and a support matrix. | A clean rebuild, verified rights and intended uses, and published evidence for each supported sport and endpoint. |

Commercial distribution, generalized multi-sport claims and hardware investment are long-term outcomes. They depend on measured benefits, reference quality, applicable permissions and a defensible funding case. The [next milestones](next-milestones.md) track the current research gates.

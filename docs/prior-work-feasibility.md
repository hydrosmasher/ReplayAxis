# ReplayAxis prior work and feasibility brief

**Scope:** Research only. The next deliverable is a defensible feasibility case based on the literature and existing evidence. Prototype work and procurement are deferred.

## Feasibility statement to test

For a bounded capture volume, calibrated synchronized views can reconstruct a visible body landmark or tracked object in 3D. The same timestamps can drive a spatial replay and expose the source observations behind its measurements. Speech and pointing can select which event, track or metric to inspect. Each capability has precedent. The research question is whether they can be combined into a reproducible, auditable measurement workflow under the error tolerances and conditions of a specific sport.

Free-viewpoint rendering, metric reconstruction, and forecasting are different outputs. A convincing synthetic view is visual evidence about a rendering method. A validated angle or trajectory requires a reference measurement. Motion beyond all camera observations is conditional prediction. Two different future outcomes can share the exact same observed history, so a unique, guaranteed continuation cannot be inferred in general.

## What a theoretical feasibility case needs

1. **Observability.** For two calibrated, undistorted pinhole cameras, each matched pixel defines a ray. Non-degenerate intersecting rays locate the point in ideal data. With noise or calibration errors, nearly parallel rays make depth sensitive to small pixel changes. In the rectified two-camera approximation, Z = fB/d, where f is focal length in pixels, B is baseline and d is disparity. First-order depth error is approximately Z² times disparity error divided by fB. The full application needs pose and timing uncertainty, correspondence checks and rejection of poorly constrained estimates.
2. **Timing and blur.** In a simple constant-speed example, a 40 m/s ball travels 4 mm in 0.1 ms and 20 mm in 0.5 ms. These are physical displacement scales, not reconstruction errors. The latter depend on geometry, camera alignment and estimator. Exposure adds a separate blur budget. Set limits backward from the required accuracy for the actual task.
3. **Independent references.** Compare the estimates with synchronized marker-based motion capture, independent calibrated digitization, radar, or event timing as appropriate. Report coverage and uncertainty alongside error. Existing ReplayAxis simulation illustrates failure modes but does not validate hardware or sport-level accuracy.
4. **Display and interaction.** A valid 3D record can be rendered in a desktop or spatial view. An immersive display and a spoken interface add distinct requirements for latency, readability, interpretation and correction. Evaluate those only after defining what measurement and evidence must be shown.
5. **Bounded research claim.** First compare one body metric and one fast-object metric within a known volume. Give above-water/underwater and ice separate evidence gates. Keep an explicit “withheld” state for unobservable intervals and validate any forecast separately on unseen trajectories.

## Next research work

Start a reproducible evidence extraction matrix. Search IEEE Xplore, ACM DL, PubMed, Scopus or OpenAlex, and patent registers. Use separate query families for (1) 3D sports capture and validation, (2) free-viewpoint rendering, (3) speech and gesture in spatial sports review, and (4) metric provenance and uncertainty. Follow references in both directions and record search dates, exact queries, screening decisions and coverage limits. There is no claim of exhaustive coverage from this first targeted brief.

For each close source, extract sport/task, camera setup, synchronization, reference standard, sample size, metric and units, uncertainty, failure modes, and whether the raw evidence can reproduce a selected number. Prioritize full-text extraction for Pose2Sim, the long-jump counterexample, Canon/Grau, and Lee et al. Then write a bounded feasibility statement with explicit conditions and testable thresholds. Use it to decide whether a later capture trial and budget proposal are justified.
